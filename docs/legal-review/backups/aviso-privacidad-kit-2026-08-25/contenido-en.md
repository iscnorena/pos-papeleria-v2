## The only thing we do record

When you use "Search for free" in Arrange Printing (stock photo search), we record your
IP address solely to limit how many searches you can make in a short time and prevent
abuse of the service. That IP is automatically deleted after **7** days; it is not used
for any other purpose.

## Transfers

That same search sends your search term (not your IP or any other data of yours) to the
stock photo providers Unsplash, Pexels, or Pixabay to bring you results. We do not share
your files, your IP, or any other personal data with anyone else.
